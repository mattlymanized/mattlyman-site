## 1. How can teams build trust to maintain consistent brand messaging?
- **Show:** Straight To Voicemail (Share Your Genius channel), episode 66
- **Date:** Sep 23, 2025. **Length:** 5 min. **Host:** Rachel Elsts-Downey
- **Matt's title then:** VP of Marketing, Flosum
- **Link:** https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/how-can-teams-build-trust-to-maintain-consistent-brand/id1648953137?i=1000728017889
- **Summary:** Brand consistency is a trust problem more than a design or tone problem. Consistent messaging builds trust inside the team and with the audience. Clear documentation and a shared strategy keep a growing team aligned, and strategy that is transparent and tied to business goals gets followed. Feedback loops keep messaging relevant. The chapter list ends on a small, memorable example: inconsistent email signatures say something about a brand, and internal trust shows up as external credibility.
- **Good for talking about:** the Flosum operating manual and messaging standards; why alignment is a trust issue, not a policing issue.

## 2. Welcome to the era of B2H
- **Show:** Marketers of Technology (the description calls it "The Marketing Front Lines"; confirm which name to use)
- **Date:** Aug 27, 2025. **Length:** 33 min
- **Matt's title then:** VP of Marketing, Flosum
- **Link:** https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/welcome-to-the-era-of-b2h-w-matt-lyman/id1747223698?i=1000723724993
- **Summary:** Matt's route into marketing leadership ran through bankruptcy collections and theater. His B2H (business to human) philosophy: even in B2B, you are always selling to people, not companies. He has worked across financial services and DevOps and favors a person-centric approach built on emotional connection and authentic engagement. Listed topics include balancing multiple buyer personas, testing humorous B2B content, learning from deal losses, and borrowing emotional marketing tactics from B2C.
- **Good for talking about:** B2H as a working method, not a slogan; the playwriting and collections background as an edge.

- **Show:** Content Matters (Compose.ly), episode 13

- **Link:** https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/from-ops-to-strategy-the-rise-of-a/id1772999666?i=1000699612652
