About

Matt Lyman is a B2B marketing leader based in Seattle with 15+ years in software, specializing in demand generation and marketing operations. He has served as VP of Marketing at Flosum, VP of Demand Generation at LeanData, and Director of Demand Generation at Chef Software, where the revenue team grew about 2.7x through the company's acquisition by Progress. He founded R.I.D.E. 4 ALL, a nonprofit for inclusive recreation, and is president of the Highline Special Needs PTA.

I dig into the data, review customer calls, and talk to the people who said yes and the ones who said no, then build programs around what I find. Over 15 years in B2B SaaS, I've built demand engines at Chef, LeanData, and Flosum across paid media, ABM, SEO, events, and content.

Before software, I ran sales and marketing for a craft beer publication. I also have a degree in playwriting, and it shows. Every campaign I build starts with a story, a real one, about who the buyer is, what they're afraid of, and what changes if they solve the problem. I call it B2H, business to human, because at the end of every enterprise deal is a person making a decision. At Flosum, that meant scrapping product-first messaging and rebuilding the strategic narrative from the ground up.

The way I work: demand gen and ops as one function, not two. Clean infrastructure, reliable attribution, and pipeline numbers that Sales actually believes. I lead from alongside, not above. My job as a leader is to coach my team well enough that they can take my job, and then go find a bigger one.

The work

Flosum

Apr 2025 to Jun 2026

VP of Marketing

Rebuilt the marketing function at an enterprise Salesforce governance platform. Reported to the CEO, led a team of 5. Agency partners ran the ad accounts. I owned strategy, measurement, and creative direction.

  • Cost per paid MQL down 87% in six months. In one quarter, spend rose about 42% while paid MQLs rose 167%.
  • Meetings from paid up 467%.
  • Monthly search impressions went from 70,000 to over one million, up 1,329%, through the SEO and GEO/AEO rebuild: 120+ assets updated, FAQ rebuilds, third-party authority placements.
  • ABM across 51 named accounts and 10 AEs. Target accounts clicked at 3 to 7x the campaign average, and 7 became opportunities within five months, mostly through BDR meetings.

LeanData

Feb 2022 to Nov 2024

VP of Demand Generation

Moved the team from growth-at-all-costs to a demand model that scaled. Team of 4.

  • Marketing's share of new business pipeline went from 18% to 50%. In 2023, demand gen spend fell 41% while marketing-sourced pipeline grew 45%.
  • Enterprise went from 36% to 65% of pipeline.
  • BookIt, the scheduling product, hit 2x its ARR target in year one.
  • Lead-to-qualified-opportunity conversion rose from 2.5% to 10%+ after a lead scoring rebuild. Events returned 4:1 in pipeline. Paid inbound leads doubled while cost per demo fell by more than half.
  • Widened the audience from RevOps to sales and marketing leaders, then IT managers. Led the internal rollout of the Revenue Orchestration Platform.

Chef Software

2015 to 2022

Director of Demand Generation at exit

Ran demand gen, ops, web, and SDR teams. Team of 7. Joined the business unit leadership team after Progress acquired Chef.

  • Revenue team grew about 2.7x through the acquisition, with quarterly pipeline goals beaten by 4x or more.
  • SDR and BDR teams averaged 120%+ of quota.
  • Rebuilt Marketo from the ground up. An external auditor rated it best-in-class.
  • Marketed the training site to 10,000 active users in six months.

Earlier: G2 Web Services, marketing to financial institutions (2011 to 2015). AmericanCraftBeer.com, director of sales and marketing (2014 to 2017).
